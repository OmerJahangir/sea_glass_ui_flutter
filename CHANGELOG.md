## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `SeaGlass`, `SeaGlassSurface`, `SeaGlassButton` and `SeaGlassButton.icon`.
* `SeaGlassStyle` recipes (`reference`, `tumbled`, `quiet`), five palettes,
  rounded/capsule/disc shapes, and seeded tumble.
* `SeaGlassTheme` with inheritance and `SeaGlassMaterialTheme` for Material You.
* GPU fragment-shader body with cached geometry and a gradient fallback.
